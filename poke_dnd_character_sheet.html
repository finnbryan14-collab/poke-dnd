Classes — Building Your Character
▾
① Pick Your Type(s)
Solo type — one type; can use both Physical & Special moves of it.
Dual type — pick a style (Physical or Special), then 2 types that fit it.
Your type(s) set your move & ability pool, your resource system, and your STAB (proficiency added to attack rolls with moves of your own type).
Style Restrictions
Fighting — Physical only
Fairy, Psychic — Special only
All other types can be Physical or Special.

② Normal Subclasses
Pick a Normal Subclass to expand your move & ability potential — it shapes playstyle and personality. You start with 1; by higher levels you'll have 3 (with their proficiencies).
