Poké × D&D — your test run

Finn built these tools for our game: a dashboard for the DM, a live 3D board everyone shares, and character sheets for the players. This is a test run. Poke around, try things, and tell Finn what's confusing, broken or missing. Nothing you do here can break anything.

Before you start: unzip the folder first: right-click the zip → Extract All, then open the files from the new folder. (Opened from inside the zip, the 🗺 Board button can't find the board: that was the "file cannot be accessed" error.) Keep all the files together. Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge. You need internet: the board and live sync are online.

What's in the folder

  • poke_dnd_dm_dashboard.html
    Your control panel: the campaign, every player's sheet live, encounters, dice and messages.
  • poke_dnd_board.html
    The shared 3D map. You open it from the dashboard's 🗺 Board button.
  • poke_dnd_character_sheet.html
    The players' app. Send it to your players.
  • BOARD_GUIDE.md
    The full manual for the board. It opens in Notepad.

1. Set up (about 5 minutes, once)

  1. Double-click poke_dnd_dm_dashboard.html. It opens in your browser.
  2. Click Create a New Campaign. This is meant to be one campaign for the whole game, not one per session: scenes, models and characters all stay in it. If you'd rather keep test stuff separate, make a test campaign now and the real one later.
  3. Click ✉ Copy invite at the top and send it to the group (or just Finn for the test). Each player opens their sheet, taps No campaign, pastes the ID with their name, and taps Join & Sync. Their character shows up on your dashboard.
  4. Click 🗺 Board. The board opens in a new tab; keep it open while you play.

Good to know

  • Everything saves by itself. Close the tabs and reopen the dashboard later: it remembers your campaign.
  • It's all free. The Ask Claude instead buttons are optional extras; the built-in ✨ designer needs nothing.
  • Players can only move their own tokens and companions. You can move everything.
  • The world is medieval fantasy, and the creatures are Pokémon. Ask the designer for "wolves" and you'll get Poochyena.
